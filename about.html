What to expect

What Customers Can Expect from Our Repair Service

Appliance-Specific Inspection

The technician checks the appliance model and investigates the systems connected to the reported problem. The purpose of the inspection is to identify the fault rather than assume that a commonly replaced component is responsible.

Different faults can create similar symptoms. For example, a washing machine that does not spin may have a drainage problem, an unbalanced load, a door-lock fault or a problem with the drive system. Testing helps separate these possibilities.

On-Site Repairs Where Practical

Washing machines, refrigerators, air conditioners, dishwashers, dryers, ovens and cooking ranges are generally inspected where they are installed.

A workshop visit may be necessary when the repair requires specialist test equipment, prolonged observation or work that cannot be completed safely inside the property. The reason for removing the appliance should be explained before collection is arranged.

Parts Matched to the Appliance Model

Any replacement component should be selected according to the full model code and the confirmed fault. The proposed part and its cost are explained before installation.

The service record states the genuine Samsung part supplied, with its part description, so the customer has a written record of what went into the appliance.

Approval Before Major Work

The customer receives an explanation of the fault and the proposed repair before major work begins. The estimate may include diagnosis, labour, parts and any workshop or transportation charges that apply.

If additional damage is discovered during the repair, the revised work and cost should be discussed before proceeding.

Testing After Repair

The repaired function is tested before the service is considered complete. Depending on the appliance, this may include checking cooling, heating, water filling, drainage, spinning, drum movement, door operation, ignition or control-panel response.

Testing confirms whether the original symptom has been resolved at the time of the visit. It does not guarantee that an unrelated component will not fail later.

Written Service Information

The invoice or service record should identify the appliance, the reported problem, the work completed and any part supplied.

Where a repair is covered by a three-month warranty, the covered work, covered component and relevant exclusions should be stated clearly on the invoice. The warranty should not be presented as applying to unrelated faults or damage outside the original repair.
