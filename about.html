Who we are

Appliance engineers, not general handymen

We work on one manufacturer's appliances all day, which is the whole point. A fault that takes a general repair shop three visits to trace is usually named on the first, because we have seen that symptom on that model before.

The workshop does board-level repair rather than defaulting to a replacement part, so a control board with two failed relays gets the relays — not a new board and a bill to match. Where a part genuinely has to be replaced, it comes from Samsung-approved supply and the part number goes on your invoice.

Everything is quoted before it is started. If we cannot fix it, we say so and you pay the diagnostic fee, not a repair bill.

Our promise

Fixed price agreed before work starts, 90 days on labour and parts, and a free revisit if the same fault returns.

Our parts

Samsung-approved components with the source printed on the invoice. Discontinued parts are flagged, never quietly substituted.

Our pricing

Itemised parts and labour. No call-out fee when you go ahead with the repair, and no charge for a quote you decline.
