In detail

Explore Samsung Services in the UAE

The faults we are called out for most, the parts behind them, and the error codes each one throws.

1. Samsung Washing Machine Repair, Dubai

Front-load machines that have stopped mid-cycle, refuse to spin, or leak onto the floor.

  • Common faults: drum not rotating, water not draining, laundry still soaked at the end of a cycle, detergent left undissolved in the drawer, burning smell under load.
  • What we do: drain pump and filter service, drum bearing replacement, door interlock repair, motherboard repair, drum alignment after a bearing job.
  • Error codes we clear: 3E, 4E, 5E, DC, LE, UE and the F-series board faults.

2. Samsung Top Load Washer Repair, Sharjah

Vertical-axis machines, where the fault is almost always agitation or suspension balance.

  • Common faults: cycle stops part way, tub overfills or barely fills, loud knocking through the spin, machine shifting position on the floor.
  • What we do: clutch assembly replacement, inlet valve service, suspension rod renewal, water level sensor calibration.
  • Error codes we clear: UE and UB unbalance faults, plus fill and drain timeouts.

3. Samsung Refrigerator Repair, Abu Dhabi

Cooling and frost diagnostics on single-door, double-door and side-by-side models.

  • Common faults: fridge compartment warm while the freezer holds, ice building up on the rear panel, water on the floor, repeated clicking from the back.
  • What we do: inverter compressor replacement, defrost timer and heater repair, evaporator fan renewal, sealed-system leak repair and regas.
  • Error codes we clear: 5E and 5C defrost sensor faults, 22E fan motor faults, and display board errors.

4. Samsung Microwave Oven Repair, Ajman

Solo, grill and convection microwaves that have stopped heating or stopped powering on.

  • Common faults: turns on but food stays cold, arcing inside the cavity, turntable not rotating, keypad partly unresponsive, door not latching.
  • What we do: magnetron and high-voltage capacitor replacement, mica sheet renewal, door interlock switch repair, touchpad replacement.
  • Error codes we clear: SE sensor errors and C-d0 stuck-key faults on Samsung keypads.

5. Samsung Dishwasher Repair, Ras Al Khaimah

Drainage and wash-performance failures on built-in and freestanding dishwashers.

  • Common faults: plates coming out gritty or still wet, water standing in the base, detergent cup staying shut, grinding or humming from the pump.
  • What we do: circulation pump clearing, drain hose and non-return valve replacement, heating element renewal, spray arm and filter service.
  • Error codes we clear: LC and LE leak checks, 4C and 4E water supply faults, 5C and 5E drain faults.

6. Samsung Cooking Range & Oven Repair, UAE

Gas and electric ranges, brought back to a temperature you can actually bake to.

  • Common faults: oven never reaches the set temperature, one side browning faster than the other, gas smell on ignition, electric elements staying cold.
  • What we do: igniter and bake element replacement, thermostat and temperature sensor calibration, gas valve service, door seal renewal.
  • Error codes we clear: C-21 temperature-rise faults and door lock failures left over from a self-clean cycle.

Gas work is carried out to the safety procedure for the model — we will not leave a range connected if the valve or hose fails a check.

7. Samsung Tumble Dryer Repair, Umm Al Quwain

Dryers that tumble happily and still hand you damp clothes.

  • Common faults: two or three cycles needed per load, cabinet getting too hot, drum not turning, start button not engaging, condenser tray filling too fast.
  • What we do: drive belt, thermal fuse and heating element replacement, thermistor testing, condenser and duct cleaning, door switch repair.
  • Error codes we clear: HE heating errors, tE thermistor errors and dE door errors.

Questions

Samsung Service Center FAQs

Why is my Samsung washing machine not spinning?

Nine times out of ten it is drainage rather than the motor: the machine will not spin until it has emptied, so a blocked pump filter, a kinked drain hose or a failed drain pump stops the cycle silently. The other common causes are a worn door interlock (the machine will not risk spinning if it cannot confirm the door is shut) and an unbalanced load triggering a UE fault. A quick filter clean is worth trying before you call.

How much does Samsung fridge repair cost in the UAE?

It depends on which part of the system has failed, so we quote after the diagnosis rather than over the phone. A thermostat, defrost heater or fan motor sits at the low end; an inverter compressor or a sealed-system gas leak is the expensive end because the work is longer and the part is dearer. You get the itemised figure before we open anything, and the call-out is waived if you go ahead with the repair.

How long does a repair take?

Most visits are done in 45 to 90 minutes because the vans carry the parts that fail most often. Jobs that need a part ordered in — a specific control board, a compressor for an older model — take a second visit, normally within two to three working days. We will tell you which of the two you are looking at before we leave the first appointment.

Do you use genuine Samsung parts?

Yes — components come from Samsung-approved supply, and the part number and source are written on your invoice so you can check it. Where a genuine part is discontinued for an older model we will say so and offer the OEM-equivalent instead, with the difference explained before you decide.

Is the repair covered by a warranty?

Every repair carries 90 days on our labour and on any part we supply. If the same fault comes back inside that window, the revisit is free. Note that a repair by a third party can affect a manufacturer warranty that is still running — if your appliance is still in its Samsung warranty period, tell us and we will point you to the right route first.

Do you repair at home or take the appliance away?

At home, in almost every case — including board-level work, which we do on the bench in the van. An appliance only comes to the workshop when the repair needs equipment we cannot bring to a flat, such as a sealed-system rebuild on a side-by-side refrigerator. Collection and return are arranged with you and the building in advance.
