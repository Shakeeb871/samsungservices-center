What to expect

What Customers Can Expect from Our Repair Service

Appliance-Specific Inspection

The technician checks the appliance model and investigates the systems connected to the reported problem. The purpose of the inspection is to identify the fault rather than assume that a commonly replaced component is responsible.

Different faults can create similar symptoms. For example, a washing machine that does not spin may have a drainage problem, an unbalanced load, a door-lock fault or a problem with the drive system. Testing helps separate these possibilities.

On-Site Repairs Where Practical

Washing machines, refrigerators, air conditioners, dishwashers, dryers, ovens and cooking ranges are generally inspected where they are installed.

A workshop visit may be necessary when the repair requires specialist test equipment, prolonged observation or work that cannot be completed safely inside the property. The reason for removing the appliance should be explained before collection is arranged.

Parts Matched to the Appliance Model

Any replacement component should be selected according to the full model code and the confirmed fault. The proposed part and its cost are explained before installation.

The service record should state whether the component supplied is a genuine Samsung part or a compatible replacement. A compatible part should not be described as genuine.

Approval Before Major Work

The customer receives an explanation of the fault and the proposed repair before major work begins. The estimate may include diagnosis, labour, parts and any workshop or transportation charges that apply.

If additional damage is discovered during the repair, the revised work and cost should be discussed before proceeding.

Testing After Repair

The repaired function is tested before the service is considered complete. Depending on the appliance, this may include checking cooling, heating, water filling, drainage, spinning, drum movement, door operation, ignition or control-panel response.

Testing confirms whether the original symptom has been resolved at the time of the visit. It does not guarantee that an unrelated component will not fail later.

Written Service Information

The invoice or service record should identify the appliance, the reported problem, the work completed and any part supplied.

Where a repair is covered by a three-month warranty, the covered work, covered component and relevant exclusions should be stated clearly on the invoice. The warranty should not be presented as applying to unrelated faults or damage outside the original repair.

How it works

How Our Appliance Repair Process Works

  1. 01

    Appliance and Fault Details

    The service request records the appliance type, model number, location and reported problem. A visible error message, photograph or short video may provide useful information before the inspection.

    This information supports preparation for the visit but does not replace physical diagnosis.

  2. 02

    Inspection and Testing

    The technician examines the appliance and tests the functions connected to the reported symptom. The inspection may include visual checks, electrical measurements, functional tests and review of the appliance model information.

    The purpose is to identify the cause of the fault and determine whether a repair is technically and economically reasonable.

  3. 03

    Findings and Estimate

    The technician explains the fault found, the proposed work and any part that may be required. An estimate is provided before major work begins.

    Where the diagnosis remains uncertain without further dismantling or testing, this should be explained rather than presented as a confirmed repair.

  4. 04

    Repair and Part Replacement

    Approved work is completed using a component matched to the appliance model. The part description and cost should be recorded on the service documentation.

    If the repair reveals an additional problem, the new finding and any change in cost should be discussed before further work is carried out.

  5. 05

    Functional Testing

    The appliance is tested after repair to confirm that the affected function operates correctly. The exact test depends on the appliance and may include a wash cycle, drainage test, temperature check, heating test, cooling check, ignition test or control-panel test.

  6. 06

    Service Record and Warranty Terms

    The service record identifies the work completed and any part supplied. Care instructions may also be provided where they are relevant to the repaired fault.

    A three-month warranty applies only when it is stated on the invoice. The document should identify what is covered, the start date of coverage and any reasonable exclusions.

Pricing

Samsung Appliance Repair Costs

The cost of repairing a Samsung appliance depends on the appliance type, the fault found, the time required and the replacement component.

Cleaning a blockage, correcting an adjustment or repairing a minor connection will generally cost less than replacing a motor, compressor, electronic control board or major heating component. Similar symptoms can result from different faults, so a reliable estimate is usually prepared after inspection.

A repair estimate may include:

  • A diagnostic or inspection charge
  • Labour for the approved repair
  • The cost of the replacement component
  • Workshop or transportation charges where required
  • Applicable taxes
  • Any warranty provided with the completed work

A low initial estimate should not be presented as a final price when the appliance has not yet been inspected. Any material change to the estimate should be explained before additional work proceeds.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Samsung Services Center an authorised Samsung service centre?

No. Samsung Services Center is an independent appliance repair provider and is not operated, authorised or endorsed by Samsung Electronics.

Customers who require manufacturer-warranty service or an officially authorised repair location should use Samsung Gulf’s official support channels.

Are repairs completed at the customer’s property?

Many washing machine, refrigerator, air-conditioner, dishwasher, dryer, oven and cooking-range repairs can be completed where the appliance is installed.

A workshop inspection may be recommended when specialised equipment, prolonged testing or work that cannot be completed safely at the property is required.

What replacement parts are used?

The part should be matched to the appliance’s full model code and the confirmed fault. Before installation, the customer should be told whether the proposed component is a genuine Samsung part or a compatible replacement.

The part description should also appear on the invoice or service record.

Does every repair include a three-month warranty?

A three-month warranty applies only when it is stated in the written service terms or on the invoice. Coverage should identify the repair or component included.

A warranty on one repair does not normally cover an unrelated fault, accidental damage, misuse or another component that fails later.

What does “screen no display” mean?

“Screen no display” generally means that the appliance display remains blank, does not illuminate or does not respond to the controls.

Possible causes include a loss of electrical supply, a loose or damaged connection, a failed display panel or a fault in the main electronic control system. Testing is required to determine the actual cause.

What is included in appliance maintenance?

Maintenance depends on the appliance. It may include cleaning accessible filters, checking drainage paths, inspecting door seals, reviewing airflow, checking visible electrical connections and testing basic performance.

Maintenance does not guarantee that an unrelated component will not fail in the future.

Should a faulty appliance be repaired or replaced?

Repair may be reasonable when the appliance remains in good general condition, the required part is available and the expected cost is substantially lower than replacement.

Replacement may be more appropriate when the appliance has repeated major failures, extensive corrosion or damage, unavailable parts or a repair cost close to the price of a comparable new appliance.

What should I do if the appliance is still covered by Samsung’s manufacturer warranty?

Customers with an appliance that may still be covered by Samsung’s manufacturer warranty should contact Samsung through its official UAE support channels before arranging third-party repair.

Work carried out by an independent provider may affect the manufacturer’s warranty terms.
