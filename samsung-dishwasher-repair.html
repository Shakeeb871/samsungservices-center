We Repair Every Common Samsung Dishwasher Problem

  1. Dishwasher does not start

    A Samsung dishwasher may remain unresponsive because of a power issue, damaged door lock, faulty control panel or internal wiring problem. We test the electrical supply and start circuit to locate the fault.

  2. Dishwasher does not fill with water

    A closed supply valve, blocked inlet filter, damaged hose, faulty inlet valve or sensor problem can stop water from entering the machine. Our technicians inspect the complete inlet system.

  3. Water remains at the bottom

    Standing water often points to a blocked filter, restricted drain hose, failed drain pump or control fault. We clear the drainage path and test the pump during operation.

  4. Dishes remain dirty after washing

    Poor cleaning can result from blocked spray arms, weak circulation, incorrect loading, low water temperature or a failing wash pump. We identify whether the problem comes from use, maintenance or a damaged component.

  5. Glasses come out cloudy

    Cloudy glassware may result from hard-water deposits, incorrect detergent, salt settings or poor rinsing. We inspect the dispenser, water flow and rinse system before recommending corrective work.

  6. Dishwasher leaks onto the floor

    Leaks can develop around the door seal, inlet hose, drain connection, pump housing or internal pipework. Our technicians trace the water to its source and repair the affected area.

  7. Door does not close or lock

    A misaligned door, damaged latch, worn hinge or faulty lock can prevent the programme from starting. We inspect the door assembly and restore secure closure.

  8. Spray arms do not rotate

    Food debris, mineral deposits, weak circulation or a damaged pump can stop the spray arms from turning. We clean the water channels and test circulation pressure.

  9. Dishwasher makes loud noises

    Grinding, rattling, humming and knocking can come from the pump, motor, spray arms or foreign objects inside the filter area. We identify the sound while the appliance operates.

  10. Dishes remain wet after the cycle

    Poor drying may result from a heater fault, damaged temperature sensor, incorrect programme or AutoRelease door problem. We test the drying system and related controls.

  11. Detergent dispenser does not open

    A blocked lid, damaged spring, electrical fault or control issue can leave the detergent trapped inside the dispenser. We repair or replace the mechanism according to the model.

  12. Dishwasher stops during the programme

    A cycle may stop because of drainage failure, overheating, water-supply interruption or an electronic error. We test the stage where the programme fails.

  13. Dishwasher displays an error code

    Samsung dishwashers use error codes to report drainage, water-supply, leakage, heating and communication problems. Our technicians confirm the code through physical testing before replacing parts.

  14. Dishwasher smells unpleasant

    Food debris, trapped water, a blocked filter or a restricted drain can create strong odours. We clean the affected areas and correct the cause of the stagnant water.

  15. Dishwasher trips the circuit breaker

    Water reaching an electrical component, damaged wiring, a failed heater or motor fault can trip the breaker. Stop using the appliance and arrange an inspection, since this problem carries an electrical safety risk.
