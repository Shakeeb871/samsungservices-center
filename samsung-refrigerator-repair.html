We Repair Every Common Samsung Refrigerator and Freezer Problem

  1. Refrigerator is not cooling

    A failed fan, sensor, compressor component, control board or blocked airflow path can prevent the cabinet from reaching the selected temperature. We inspect the full cooling cycle before deciding which part requires attention.

  2. Freezer is not freezing

    Soft food and melting ice often point to airflow trouble, excessive frost, a damaged door seal, sensor failure or a sealed-system fault. Our technicians test the freezer temperature and cooling response to locate the cause.

  3. Refrigerator is warm but freezer stays cold

    This problem usually develops when cold air cannot move from the freezer into the refrigerator compartment. We check the evaporator fan, air damper, vents and frost level behind the internal panel.

  4. Freezer is cold but food keeps thawing

    An unstable temperature can result from a weak compressor, faulty sensor, damaged seal or repeated defrost interruption. We monitor the cooling cycle instead of relying on a single temperature reading.

  5. Ice builds up inside the freezer

    Heavy frost can block air circulation and reduce cooling throughout the appliance. Our team checks the defrost heater, thermal fuse, sensor, drain and control system to identify why the ice keeps returning.

  6. Water leaks onto the floor

    A blocked defrost drain, cracked water line, loose connection or overflowing drain tray can cause water to collect beneath the refrigerator. We trace the leak to its source and repair the affected part.

  7. Water collects inside the refrigerator

    Moisture under the drawers or along the back wall often comes from a blocked drainage channel, damaged seal or temperature imbalance. The technician clears the restriction and checks the surrounding components.

  8. Refrigerator runs continuously

    A refrigerator that never rests may struggle with dirty coils, poor ventilation, warm air entering through the door or a cooling-system fault. We inspect both operating conditions and internal components.

  9. Refrigerator makes loud or unusual noises

    Buzzing, knocking, rattling or grinding can come from the compressor, condenser fan, evaporator fan, ice maker or loose internal parts. We identify the sound under operation before carrying out the repair.

  10. Ice maker does not produce ice

    Low water flow, a frozen inlet tube, faulty valve, damaged sensor or ice-maker assembly can stop production. Our technicians check the water supply and electrical controls before replacing the unit.

  11. Water dispenser stops working

    A blocked filter, frozen line, weak inlet pressure, failed valve or control fault can interrupt the dispenser. We test the water path from the supply connection to the outlet.

  12. Refrigerator door does not close properly

    Misaligned hinges, overloaded shelves, damaged seals or an uneven appliance can leave the door slightly open. This forces the cooling system to work harder and allows moisture to enter the cabinet.

  13. Display panel shows an error code

    Samsung refrigeration error codes can point to fan, sensor, communication, defrost or power faults. We use the code as a diagnostic clue and confirm the actual failure through testing.

  14. Refrigerator has no power

    The fault may involve the socket, cable, internal fuse, power board or main control module. Our technicians check the electrical supply and appliance circuits safely.

  15. Food freezes inside the refrigerator compartment

    A damaged temperature sensor, blocked vent, faulty damper or incorrect airflow can expose fresh food to excessive cold. We inspect the temperature-control system and restore balanced cooling.
