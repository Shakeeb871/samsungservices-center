We Repair Every Common Samsung Dryer Problem

  1. Dryer does not turn on

    A failed socket, damaged power cable, internal fuse, door switch or control board can leave the dryer unresponsive. We inspect the electrical supply and start circuit to locate the fault.

  2. Dryer turns on but does not start

    The appliance may power up without beginning the programme when the door lock, start button, motor circuit or electronic control develops a fault.

  3. Drum does not rotate

    A broken belt, damaged motor, worn pulley or seized drum component can stop movement. Our technicians inspect the complete drive system before replacing parts.

  4. Dryer runs but produces no heat

    A failed heater, heat-pump component, temperature sensor, thermal fuse or control relay can leave clothes cold and damp after the cycle.

  5. Clothes remain wet after drying

    Restricted airflow, weak heat, inaccurate moisture sensing or an overloaded drum can prevent proper drying. We test each stage of the drying process.

  6. Drying cycles take too long

    Blocked filters, a dirty condenser, poor ventilation or weak fan performance can extend drying time and increase electricity use.

  7. Dryer stops before the cycle finishes

    Overheating, motor strain, sensor errors or unstable power can interrupt the programme. We identify the stage where the dryer shuts down.

  8. Dryer becomes excessively hot

    Restricted airflow, a failed thermostat, blocked condenser or control fault can cause unsafe temperatures. Stop using the appliance until a technician inspects it.

  9. Dryer makes a squeaking noise

    Worn rollers, dry bearings, a damaged belt or pulley friction can create a high-pitched sound while the drum turns.

  10. Dryer makes a grinding or scraping sound

    Foreign objects, damaged drum supports, worn rollers or loose internal parts can produce harsh mechanical noise.

  11. Dryer gives off a burning smell

    Lint build-up, belt friction, overheated wiring or a failing motor can create a burning odour. Disconnect the appliance and arrange immediate inspection.

  12. Water tank fills too quickly

    A blocked condenser, restricted drainage path, pump fault or sensor problem can cause water to collect faster than expected.

  13. Dryer does not drain water

    A blocked hose, failed pump or internal drainage restriction can leave water inside a heat-pump or condenser dryer.

  14. Display shows an error code

    Samsung dryer error codes can point to door, motor, sensor, airflow, heating or communication faults. We confirm the code through physical testing.

  15. Dryer trips the circuit breaker

    A short circuit, damaged heater, faulty motor or moisture reaching electrical parts can trip the supply. This fault requires professional electrical inspection.

Our Samsung Dryer Services Include

Complete dryer diagnosis

We inspect heating, airflow, drum movement, sensors, drainage and electronic controls to identify the source of the fault.

Heating-system repair

Our technicians test heating elements, heat-pump components, thermal fuses, thermostats and connected wiring when the dryer produces little or no heat.

Heat-pump repair

We inspect the compressor, refrigerant circuit, heat exchanger, sensors and electronic controls used in Samsung heat-pump dryers.

Drive-belt replacement

A stretched or broken belt prevents the drum from turning correctly. We install the proper belt and inspect the surrounding drive components.

Motor repair and replacement

The motor powers the drum and airflow system. We test electrical resistance, movement and operating load before recommending repair or replacement.

Drum-roller repair

Worn rollers create noise, vibration and uneven drum movement. We inspect and replace damaged supports to restore smooth rotation.

Pulley and tensioner repair

A damaged pulley can loosen the belt, create squeaking or stop drum movement. We replace worn parts and set the correct belt tension.

Moisture-sensor replacement

Faulty moisture sensors can end a cycle too early or keep the dryer running longer than necessary. We clean, test and replace the sensors where required.

Temperature-sensor repair

An inaccurate temperature sensor can cause poor drying or excessive heat. We test the component against the dryer’s operating range.

Airflow and lint-path cleaning

Lint build-up restricts airflow, extends drying time and raises the risk of overheating. We clean accessible internal air channels and inspect the fan system.

Condenser cleaning and repair

A blocked condenser reduces heat transfer and drying performance. We clean the unit and repair damaged related components.

Drain-pump repair

Heat-pump and condenser dryers use a pump to move collected water. We clear blockages and replace failed pumps where necessary.

Door-lock and switch repair

The dryer will not start when the door switch fails or the latch does not close securely. We repair the mechanism and confirm safe operation.

Control-board and display repair

Electronic faults can interrupt cycles, show repeated error codes or leave the dryer unresponsive. We inspect the board and connected controls before replacement.

Samsung dryer installation

Our team positions the appliance, checks ventilation and drainage requirements, levels the cabinet and completes an operating test.

Preventive dryer maintenance

Maintenance includes filter cleaning, condenser inspection, airflow checks, drum assessment, sensor cleaning and temperature testing.
